About

Built to keep inference on your own hardware.

Marigold is an open source, self-hosted inference platform, built and maintained by Bay Information Systems.

Who built it

01

Bay Information Systems

A UK-based information systems consultancy. Marigold is the open source core of the self-hosted inference work Bay Information Systems does for clients directly -- published so anyone can run the same platform themselves.

02

Why it exists

Regulated and security-conscious clients kept asking for inference that never leaves their own infrastructure. Marigold is that answer, generalised and open sourced rather than rebuilt per engagement. More on the reasoning: data sovereignty.

03

Beyond the open source core

Bay Information Systems also offers paid consultation and managed deployment into a customer's own AWS or Azure account, for teams who want help rather than a from-scratch setup. See pricing.
